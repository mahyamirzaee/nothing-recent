A warning app for women under compulsory hijab that cannot keep anyone safe.

It protects no one. Every sighting inside is invented: no location, no account, no server; nothing is stored and nothing is sent. A phone can be searched, and an app like this would itself be evidence.

It opens as a stitch counter. Press and hold the row number for 1.2 seconds. Inside, time runs fast: one minute every two seconds.
